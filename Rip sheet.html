The short-print trap — cards #697 to #700

These four numbers carry two different players depending on which version you got. If the name on #697–700 is NOT Bryan Reynolds, Andre Pallante, Jared Young or Freddie Freeman, you are holding the short print. It looks like a base card. It is not.

2026 Topps Chrome Black

6 cards per pack · one parallel guaranteed in every pack

The guaranteed autograph in Chrome Black is the encased 13th card that comes with a HOBBY BOX — it is not inside the 6-card packs. Loose single packs have had that card removed already. In the packs themselves autographs run about 1:7, so across 4 packs it is closer to a coin flip than a promise.

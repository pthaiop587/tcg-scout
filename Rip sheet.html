2025 Panini Prizm Draft Picks — Collegiate Football

the 2025 draft class in college uniforms · mega box

These are college cards, not NFL rookie cards. A Prizm Draft Picks Shedeur Sanders is a pre-rookie — the player's official RC comes from a licensed NFL product. Titling one as a rookie card is the fastest way to an angry buyer and a return, and it is the single most common mistake with this set.
